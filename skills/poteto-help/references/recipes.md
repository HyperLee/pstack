# Recipes

- `$how` to trace present behavior; `$why` to establish history; `$teach` to explain both.
- `$swarm` for independent coverage; `$arena` for alternatives; `$interrogate` for proven failures.
- `$poteto-mode` with a concrete symptom and a done predicate for fixes/features.
- `$poteto-mode` plus “plan only” for a multi-phase plan without execution.
- `$show-me-your-work` for a reviewable decision trail; `$ketchup` for current-chat status.
- `$reflect` to propose durable improvements, or `$automate-me` to author a personal mode.

Use the installed guide for complete examples. Missing delegation, history or external services must be disclosed. A template is not a connected automation.
